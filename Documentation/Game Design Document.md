## 2. Product Design
### 2.1 Player Experience & Game POV
The player character is a nameless cave diver who fell into sharp crevice. The cave itself is a sprawling network. It appears like a normal cave at first, but the 
further the player goes in, the more structured it appears. This game is supposed to have an eerie and uncomfortable atmosphere. The player should become more engaged
the more "oddities" appear. The gameplay itself should be entertaining and should make the player both want to continue forward and slightly hesitant at taking risks,
much like someone exploring a new cave.
### 2.2 Visual & Audio Style
The game should primarily have a blue and black color scheme for the cave. The ruins of the civilization should have a green and black color scheme to subtly differentiate itself. Warm colors should be used to catch the player's attention, like with healing crystals or light sources.

The background music should be droning and subtle. The sound effects of the player's movement should be the loudest in the cave. 
### 2.3 Monetization
The game will not be monetized. This game will be developed to show the programming experience of the developer.
